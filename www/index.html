Still learning
I know it

Hold your phone upright and tilt it.

Swap left / right (if it feels reversed)

Tilting left ~45° counts as Still Learning; tilting right ~45° counts as I Knew It. Turn on Tilt to grade while studying a deck.

 How the axes respond Readings are in m/s² and include gravity. Lying flat, the phone reads about Z ≈ +9.8. Standing upright, Y ≈ +9.8. Tilting moves gravity from one axis to another (tilt right and X goes negative). Moving the phone briefly adds to the axis you push along. A shake makes all three swing fast and the total magnitude jump well above 9.8.
